# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HR managers and administrative staff at UGI. Internal users only — not all employees, but the staff responsible for managing employee records, compliance documentation, training logs, safety incidents, and field-work reporting. They work at desks, in Thai, with a high volume of data entry and lookup tasks.

## Product Purpose

An internal HR and compliance management system for UGI. Covers seven modules: employee records, company / org structure, training history, certificates and compliance documents, safety measures, offsite work tracking, and reporting. Success means administrators can find, update, and report on personnel and compliance data quickly without friction.

## Brand Personality

Modern, professional, approachable. Not stuffy enterprise software — feels like a well-built contemporary tool that takes its users seriously. Trustworthy without being cold. Efficient without being sterile. Three words: **capable, clear, composed**.

## Anti-references

- The generic Tailwind starter: zinc-on-white with no brand color, no character, nothing to distinguish it from a tutorial project
- Classic enterprise HR platforms (Workday, BambooHR): heavy chrome, corporate blues, cluttered sidebars, dated SaaS visual language
- Anything that reads "scaffolding" rather than "product"

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — every screen must be immediately scannable; hierarchy is non-negotiable in data-dense admin interfaces
2. **Thai-first** — typography, label density, and layout must be designed for Thai script, not retrofitted from English assumptions
3. **Brand present, not loud** — a committed brand color anchors identity without overwhelming the data on screen
4. **Trustworthy by default** — spacing, contrast, and type weight together communicate reliability; sloppy spacing erodes user confidence
5. **Efficiency is respect** — admin users are busy; minimal clicks, obvious affordances, no dead ends

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Thai text at readable minimum sizes (≥14px body, ≥16px for dense form fields). Light and dark mode both supported (already wired). Reduced motion support required for any animations.
